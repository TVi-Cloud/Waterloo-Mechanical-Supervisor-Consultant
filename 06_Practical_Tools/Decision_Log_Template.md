# 1. Purpose

This template helps Mike document important supervisory decisions and
the reasoning behind them.

It is designed for situations involving:

-   Safety decisions.
-   Operational decisions.
-   Employee matters.
-   Contractor issues.
-   Escalations.
-   Resource decisions.
-   Process improvements.
-   Unusual circumstances.

A decision log creates transparency, accountability, and organizational
learning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Principle

> **Good leaders do not only make decisions. They understand, explain,
> and learn from the decisions they make.**

The purpose of documenting a decision is not to create unnecessary
paperwork.

It is to preserve:

-   Context.
-   Reasoning.
-   Facts.
-   Consultation.
-   Follow-up.

# 19. Common Decision Errors

Claude should help prevent:

## Deciding Before Gathering Facts

Good decisions require good information.

## Ignoring Long-Term Impact

The fastest choice is not always the best choice.

## Failing to Consult

Complex decisions benefit from expertise.

## Documenting Only the Outcome

The reasoning matters.

## Making Decisions Based on Emotion

Pause and evaluate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Foundational Decision Doctrine

> **Facts create confidence.**

> **Reasoning creates accountability.**

> **Consultation strengthens decisions.**

> **Documentation preserves learning.**

> **A good decision protects people, operations, and values.**

> **Leadership is visible through the choices made under pressure.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Final Statement

A decision log is more than a record.

It is a reflection of supervisory judgment.

Mike's questions should be:

> **What do I know?**

> **What do I need to learn?**

> **Who should I involve?**

> **What values guide this decision?**

> **Can I explain why this was the right choice?**

Strong leadership is not the absence of difficult decisions.

It is the ability to make difficult decisions with clarity, integrity,
and accountability.

That is judgment becoming architecture.

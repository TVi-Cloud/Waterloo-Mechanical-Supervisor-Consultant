# 1. Purpose

This template helps Mike gather accurate information following:

-   Workplace incidents.
-   Near misses.
-   Safety concerns.
-   Equipment failures.
-   Property damage.
-   Operational disruptions.
-   Unexpected events.

This tool is for collecting facts and supporting appropriate follow-up.

It is not a replacement for formal investigations, required reporting
processes, or authorized University procedures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Principle

> **Gather facts first. Conclusions come later.**

The purpose of fact gathering is to understand:

-   What happened.
-   What conditions existed.
-   What actions were taken.
-   What information is needed next.

# 22. Documentation Standards

Good documentation:

-   Uses facts.
-   Uses specific descriptions.
-   Records actions taken.
-   Identifies uncertainty.

Avoid:

-   "Worker was careless."
-   "Employee did not care."
-   "They should have known."

Document behaviour and conditions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Foundational Fact Gathering Doctrine

> **Facts create understanding.**

> **Understanding creates improvement.**

> **Good investigations begin with good information.**

> **A supervisor's first responsibility is to understand before
> deciding.**

> **Documentation protects people, processes, and organizations.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Final Statement

Every incident provides information.

The quality of the response depends on the quality of the facts
collected.

Mike's questions should be:

> **What happened?**

> **What do we know?**

> **What do we need to learn?**

> **What actions are required now?**

> **How do we prevent recurrence?**

That is learning becoming architecture.

# 1. Purpose

This checklist helps Mike assign work clearly, safely, and effectively.

It is designed to support:

-   Daily work planning.
-   Maintenance activities.
-   Priority assignments.
-   Safety considerations.
-   Clear expectations.
-   Follow-up and verification.

A strong work assignment ensures employees understand:

-   What needs to be done.
-   Why it matters.
-   How it should be completed.
-   What support is available.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Principle

> **Clear work assignments create safer work, better results, and
> stronger teams.**

A supervisor should not only assign tasks.

A supervisor should create the conditions for success.

# 19. Final Statement

Assigning work is more than distributing tasks.

It is an opportunity to demonstrate:

-   Leadership.
-   Preparation.
-   Safety awareness.
-   Respect.
-   Accountability.

Mike's questions should be:

> **Does the employee understand the task?**

> **Is the work safe?**

> **Do they have what they need?**

> **What support is required?**

> **How will we know the work was successful?**

That is operational clarity becoming architecture.

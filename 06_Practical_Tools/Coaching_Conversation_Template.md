# 1. Purpose

This template helps Mike prepare and conduct effective coaching
conversations with employees.

It is designed for:

-   Skill development.
-   Performance improvement.
-   Expectation clarification.
-   Safety coaching.
-   Professional growth.
-   Behaviour discussions.

Coaching is a leadership tool designed to help people succeed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Principle

> **The purpose of coaching is not to criticize people. The purpose is
> to help people understand expectations, overcome barriers, and
> improve.**

Effective coaching balances:

-   Support.
-   Accountability.
-   Respect.
-   Clear expectations.
-   Follow-up.

# 16. Common Coaching Mistakes

Claude should help prevent:

## Being Too Vague

"Do better" is not an actionable expectation.

## Making It Personal

Address behaviour, not character.

## Only Giving Feedback

Effective coaching includes listening.

## Failing to Follow Up

Improvement requires continued support.

## Avoiding Difficult Conversations

Unaddressed issues usually grow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Foundational Coaching Doctrine

> **People improve when expectations are clear.**

> **Feedback is a gift when delivered with respect.**

> **Accountability and support work together.**

> **A supervisor develops people, not just manages tasks.**

> **The best coaching creates confidence and competence.**

> **Every conversation is an opportunity to strengthen the team.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. Final Statement

Coaching is one of the most important responsibilities of leadership.

Mike's questions should be:

> **What does this person need to succeed?**

> **Are expectations clear?**

> **What barriers exist?**

> **What support can I provide?**

> **How will we know improvement occurred?**

Strong supervisors do not simply identify problems.

They help build capable, confident teams.

That is development becoming architecture.
